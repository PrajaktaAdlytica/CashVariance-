Concept preview boundaries

Clear boundaries for a concept preview.

Explore the concept boundaries

Preview notice · 14 October 2026

01 / Status

Research, proposed modules and a browser demonstration.

CashVariance is presented as a research-stage concept. Product descriptions explain intended workflows, not a promise of current availability. This notice describes the website preview’s boundaries; it is not a finalized commercial agreement.

02 / Demonstration

Every record and outcome is illustrative.

People, organizations, account identifiers, transactions, source documents and review decisions in the demo are fictional. A completed review or downloaded memo is a synthetic illustration and cannot be treated as a certified cash position or real approval.

03 / Execution

No funds move and no journals post.

The preview has no bank rails, payment initiation, payment-file transmission or ERP write capability. Draft journals and intercompany proposals remain illustrative. Nothing shown here substitutes for the company’s existing treasury, accounting or payment controls.

04 / Commercial scope

Test prices are hypotheses.

The $3,000 diagnostic and $1,500–$5,000 monthly entity-group range are hypothetical prices. There is no checkout, accepted quote or purchase commitment. Any real pilot would require a separately agreed scope, parties, data handling and commercial terms.

05 / Before a live service

Finalize the actual agreement before onboarding.

Operator identity, governing terms, service responsibilities, support, data handling and liability provisions need to be established for any real pilot. This preview does not fabricate those details or imply that a live agreement already exists.
