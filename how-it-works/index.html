The proposed workflow

From dated sources to a recorded decision.

Compare the records. Inspect the explanation. Let a named reviewer record what is known and what remains open.

Follow the synthetic exampleResearch-stage concept · No autonomous fund movement
01 / Establish the basis

Start with records you can place in time.

A proposed pilot begins with permissioned historical bank statements, GL exports and daily position files. Confirm the entity, currency, balance basis and source cutoff before comparing amounts.

  • One entity across 20 daily closes
  • Explicit bank and GL source timestamps
  • Mapping changes remain proposals for review
02 / Compare

Keep the difference in view.

Separate matched items from unexplained amounts and missing source coverage. An old feed remains old even when the transactions within it have been reviewed. The queue should show both the exception and the freshness of its evidence.

03 / Inspect

An equal amount is a clue, not a match.

Inspect the source row, transaction identity and counterparty evidence behind a candidate. The synthetic example includes an $8,000 suggestion that is rejected because its source is already matched to a different transaction.

  • Read the source beside the suggestion
  • Accept, reject or hold with a reason
  • Keep rejected suggestions in the review history
04 / Record

Close the review without concealing what is open.

A dated memo records the reviewer, observed difference, evidence state and next action. Accepting an explanation does not post a journal. A held exception can appear in a reviewed memo without becoming a reconciled cash position.
