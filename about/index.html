About CashVariance

A considered approach to the daily close.

CashVariance is a research-stage concept for reviewable treasury exception decisions. Start narrow. Establish evidence. Earn further scope.

Discuss the conceptResearch-stage concept · No autonomous fund movement
Our focus

Explain the difference before the next decision.

Treasury managers compare bank records, GL entries and intercompany evidence to understand a daily position. CashVariance explores a workspace that keeps those sources, explanations and review decisions together.

The principle

Human owners retain authority.

The vision is a reviewable, evidence-linked process rather than an opaque action. Proposed classifications, journal treatments and next steps remain visible and reversible. Reviewers retain responsibility for what they accept, reject or hold.

The starting point

One entity is enough to ask a serious question.

The proposed initial scope is 20 daily closes, read-only statement and GL exports, an unmatched queue and a reviewer-signed position memo. It excludes bank rails and payment initiation. Its value must be demonstrated against the actual current workflow.

Where we are

An honest concept preview.

The product shown here is synthetic and isolated. Customer demand, production integrations, pricing acceptance and improved exception closure have not been demonstrated. The next useful step is a qualified conversation and a carefully bounded evaluation.
