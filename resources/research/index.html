Research notes / 14 October 2026

Separate what is known from what needs testing.

Established workflows are evidence of overlap. Better exception closure, buyer demand and review economics remain hypotheses to evaluate.

Cash visibility and reconciliation are established jobs.

Trovata describes direct bank connectivity and normalized cash data. Kyriba describes cash positioning, matching and liquidity workflows. These public capabilities establish an adjacent category, not an unserved market or a CashVariance advantage.

CashVariance hypothesis

A focused exception layer may earn its place.

The proposed wedge is a narrow evidence-and-review loop around the daily difference. It must reproduce closes and improve the agreed current process without concealing stale data. Human oversight or an AI label alone is not evidence of differentiation.

Open questions

Access and review economics are still unknown.

Segment-specific manual hours, buyer budget, file access terms and tolerance for false suggestions remain unverified. Security reviews, segregation of duties, liability for incorrect positions and large incumbent coverage are material evaluation questions.

  • How many recurring exceptions need manual review?
  • Which source files can legally and practically be shared?
  • What review effort does each suggestion create?
  • What evidence leads to a paid-pilot commitment?
Expansion boundary

Earn each next workflow separately.

Forecasting and multi-entity liquidity are conditional directions. A 20-close reconciliation result does not validate alert quality, bilateral confirmation or payment execution. Bank rails are not assumed. The research must lead to a clear stop decision when value or access is absent.
