Confirm file coverage, timestamps, mapping and permissioned handling. Real financial records do not belong in this public preview.

File coverage

Bring the records that formed the daily position.

A proposed initial evaluation needs historical statements, GL cash exports and daily position files for one entity across 20 closes. Confirm whether exports contain transaction-level references as well as opening and closing balances.

  • Statement date, masked account and balance basis
  • GL account, posting date and source reference
  • Historical daily position and sign-off record
Time and mapping

Make comparisons on a stated basis.

Document entity, account and currency mappings. Record source cutoff, file receipt and expected update independently. Currency conversion, when relevant, needs an explicit rate source and date; do not silently combine unlike balances.

  • Entity and account identifiers
  • Currency and ledger-versus-available basis
  • Bank and GL timestamps with time zones
  • Known gaps, duplicates and revised files
Evidence by module

Request only what the selected job needs.

Cash Review needs transaction and classification evidence. Cash Proof needs explicit inflow timing, outflows and buffer assumptions. Cash Signal needs an approved-file version and a payment-control owner. Cash Report needs authorized evidence from the counterparty as well as the first entity.

Authorization

Agree the route before sharing the records.

Confirm bank and ERP sharing terms, data ownership and the people permitted to review the files. Transfer method, access controls, storage, retention and deletion must be agreed through the pilot’s security review. Do not attach financial records to a public inquiry.

  • Treasurer sponsor and payment-control owner
  • Documented permission for the sample
  • Read-only scope and security approval
  • Retention and deletion responsibilities
