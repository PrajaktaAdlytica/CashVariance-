Evaluation method

Define improvement before measuring it.

The pilot scorecard sets out closure definitions, raw counts, reviewer time, false positives and omissions. It keeps explained-but-unposted amounts separate from actual resolution. Agree thresholds before the first close.

  • Same historical inputs as the current process
  • Treasurer-adjudicated outcomes
  • Visible exclusions and inconclusive results
Data preparation

Check the basis of every comparison.

The data checklist covers statement and GL exports, source dates, currency, account mapping and permissioned handling. It is a readiness guide, not a public upload form.

  • 20 daily closes for one entity
  • Source cutoff and expected arrival
  • Sharing rights, transfer and retention to agree
Research and sources

Distinguish category evidence from product evidence.

Official vendor pages establish existing cash and reconciliation workflows. They do not demonstrate demand for CashVariance or better exception closure. The research page records that distinction and the questions still open.

Synthetic example

Inspect a difference that remains visible.

The fictional North Quay Group example puts a $240,000 bank balance beside a $232,000 GL balance. Inspect the stale source, the $8,000 candidate explanation and the review decision without sharing any company data.
