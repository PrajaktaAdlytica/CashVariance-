Why the daily position stays open

The numbers disagree for a reason.

Cutoff times, mappings and missing confirmations can leave a daily position unresolved. The useful question is what evidence would explain the difference.

Explore the review workflowResearch-stage concept · No autonomous fund movement
Time

Two balances may describe two different moments.

A bank snapshot and a GL export can have different cutoffs or arrival times. Before treating a difference as an accounting issue, establish what each record covers and whether an expected update is missing.

  • Source cutoff and file receipt time are separate facts
  • Ledger balance and available balance need a stated basis
  • A stale warning belongs beside the reported position
Identity

The reference matters as much as the amount.

Similar amounts do not establish a transaction match. Source identity, value dates, posting dates and account mapping help a reviewer distinguish a valid explanation from a false candidate. Reusing a matched source creates another error rather than closing the first one.

Handoffs

The answer may sit across several systems.

Bank portals, treasury management systems, ERPs, spreadsheets and service providers all participate in the daily close. CashVariance is a proposed exception layer alongside those tools. Its value must be measured against the actual process already in place.

The question to test

Across 20 closes, what still needed a manual explanation?

Establish the current exception volume, reviewer time, missing data and final dispositions. A narrow diagnostic can then test whether a review workspace adds useful evidence or simply duplicates work.

  • Who signs the daily position?
  • Which exception types repeat?
  • Which missing confirmation prevents a decision?
  • What would justify paying for another review layer?
