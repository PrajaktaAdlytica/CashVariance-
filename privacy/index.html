Website preview notice

Know what this preview asks you to share.

This website preview has no connected inquiry backend. Forms prepare a draft in your browser. Use sample contact details and keep bank records, credentials and personal financial information out of the forms.

Preview notice · 14 October 2026

Current scope

A prepared draft is not a delivered inquiry.

The inquiry pages prepare a text draft that you may download. They do not send submissions to a sales, support or CRM service. The confirmation means that the draft is ready; it does not mean an operator has received a message.

Browser handling

Your entries stay in the current page.

Form values and the prepared draft stay in browser memory and clear on reload. Optional browser-only usage events are off by default; enable them through Usage preferences in the footer. They describe page visits, module selections and preview interactions, with no email addresses, inquiry text or raw financial records. There is no network analytics collector, cookie or local-storage persistence. The usage preference resets on reload. A draft you choose to download remains on your device until you delete it.

Synthetic product data

The demo begins with invented records.

North Quay Group, its people, accounts, transactions and review outcomes are fictional. No real bank or ERP connection is present. The demo does not require credentials or financial uploads, and any local memo export must retain its synthetic-data label.

Planned collection

A future inquiry should ask for only what it needs.

The intended intake is limited to business contact details, role, company, selected module and a non-sensitive question. Request-specific consent and an optional updates opt-in should be separate. Raw statements, account data and financial records do not belong in public forms.

Before collection

Operational details must be established first.

The legal operator, contact destination, retention schedule, processors, rights request route and any analytics choices must be finalized and disclosed before real lead collection is enabled. This preview is not a finalized privacy policy for a live service.

  • Identify the actual data controller and contact route
  • Specify storage, processors and retention
  • Provide a working correction and deletion route
  • Explain any analytics and consent choices
