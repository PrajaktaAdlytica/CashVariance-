CASHVARIANCE ASSET PROVENANCE

Original project assets: redrawn CashVariance logo contours, original treasury line icons, diagram layouts, pattern tiles, page compositions, CSS and copy generated for this project. Logo wordmark outlines derive from Newsreader at weight 450 and optical size 48. Included vectors are editable paths, not embedded bitmap traces.

Fonts: downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository on 14 October 2026. Original TTF files and converted WOFF2 files included with original SIL Open Font License notices. No font family was renamed; WOFF2 conversion changes format only. Keep notices when redistributing.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono

Background art: two newly generated raster illustrations produced with the built-in image-generation tool. PNG originals and WebP derivatives. They do not depict real company premises, team members or customers. The original generation prompts are in the accompanying CashVariance brand kit.

LinkedIn and Crunchbase social symbols: unchanged geometry from Simple Icons v11.15.0. Simple Icons assets are distributed under CC0; brand names and marks remain their owners' trademarks, and that source license is not a grant of endorsement or trademark rights. The website retains these source assets, but does not render social-profile links until genuine profile URLs are supplied. No real company-profile destinations are supplied or fabricated. These assets are separate from the original CashVariance icon set.
